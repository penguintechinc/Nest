Trino Helm chart for Nest.

This chart documents the Trino version (435) and resource allocations used
by the Nest k8s-controller when provisioning warehouse/trino DataResources.

The actual Trino deployment is created and managed by the k8s-controller
via the reconcileTrino() function, not by this Helm chart.

Resource allocations per environment:
- Alpha: 1 coordinator, 1 worker (minimal)
- Beta: 1 coordinator, 2 workers
- Prod: 1 coordinator, 4 workers
